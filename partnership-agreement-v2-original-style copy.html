ARTICLE II – RECITALS & BACKGROUND

  1. Vook Tech India Private Limited is engaged in providing technology-based Software as a Service (SaaS) solutions for enterprises.
  2. The Company has developed and intends to commercially operate a cloud-based Human Resource Information System ("HRIS") ERP platform comprising modules for Attendance Management, Leave Management, and Office Management (collectively, the "Platform" or "Software").
  3. Party B is a skilled individual software developer and technology professional with expertise in full-stack development, system architecture, quality assurance, and software operations.
  4. The Company wishes to engage Party B as an Individual Contributor — on a non-employment, non-equity basis — to develop, maintain, service, and evolve the Platform for a period of fifteen (15) years from the Effective Date, in consideration of a defined revenue partnership share as set out herein.
  5. The Parties expressly agree and acknowledge that this Agreement does not create any employer-employee relationship, nor does it confer any equity, shareholding, directorship, or ownership rights in the Company upon Party B.

ARTICLE III – DEFINITIONS

  1. "Platform / Software" — means the SaaS ERP–HRIS system comprising Attendance Management, Leave Management, and Office Management modules, together with all future versions, updates, enhancements, and related services developed under this Agreement.
  2. "MVP" (Minimum Viable Product) — means the first production-ready release of the Platform encompassing agreed core functionalities of all three HRIS modules.
  3. "Version Release" — means each subsequent numbered release of the Platform after the MVP, including major (e.g. v2.0), minor (e.g. v1.1), and patch (e.g. v1.0.1) releases.
  4. "Software Services" — means all development, enhancement, bug-fixing, maintenance, hosting support, UAT co-ordination, deployment, and version release activities performed by Party B in respect of the Platform.
  5. "Net Revenue" — means gross revenues actually collected by the Company from subscription fees, implementation fees, and SaaS license fees attributable to the Platform, after deduction of applicable taxes (GST etc.), payment gateway charges, and refunds.
  6. "Partnership Share" — means the Twenty Five percent (25%) share of Net Revenue payable to Party B as defined in Article V, which constitutes a contractual revenue-sharing arrangement and does not confer any equity, ownership, or partnership interest in the Company.
  7. "Agreement Term" — means a period of fifteen (15) years commencing on the Effective Date, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article XI.
  8. "Deliverables" — means all source code, binaries, database schemas, API documentation, deployment scripts, test artefacts, and other software outputs produced by Party B under this Agreement.
  9. "Intellectual Property" — means all patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, source code, algorithms, and related proprietary rights.
  10. "Confidential Information" — means any non-public technical, commercial, financial, or strategic information disclosed by either Party in connection with this Agreement.

ARTICLE V – SCOPE OF WORK & RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARTY B

Party B agrees to perform the following Software Services exclusively for the Platform throughout the entire fifteen (15) year Agreement Term:

5.1 Software Design & Development

  1. Design and develop the complete SaaS ERP–HRIS Platform comprising: (a) Attendance Management Module — biometric/manual tracking, shift management, real-time reporting, payroll integration hooks; (b) Leave Management Module — application workflows, approval hierarchies, balance calculations, holiday calendars, compliance reports; (c) Office Management Module — asset tracking, visitor management, facility booking, announcements, and organisational directory.
  2. Design system architecture including database schema, API structure (RESTful/GraphQL), and cloud infrastructure setup.
  3. Develop front-end interfaces (web and/or mobile) and back-end services ensuring scalability, security, and multi-tenancy.

5.2 Testing & Quality Assurance

  1. Conduct unit testing, integration testing, performance testing, and security testing for all modules and every Version Release throughout the Agreement Term.
  2. Prepare and execute UAT test plans in co-ordination with stakeholders nominated by the Company for each release.
  3. Document all defects and resolutions in a mutually agreed issue-tracking system.
  4. Ensure OWASP Top-10 compliance, data encryption at rest and in transit, and role-based access control (RBAC) at all times.

ARTICLE VI – REVENUE PARTNERSHIP SHARE (COMPENSATION)

Partnership Share: In full and complete consideration for all Software Services rendered by Party B under this Agreement over the fifteen (15) year term, Vook Tech India Private Limited shall pay Party B a Revenue Partnership Share of twenty five percent (25%) of Net Revenue generated from the Platform, payable as set out in this Article. This is a contractual revenue-share and does not represent equity, ownership, profit-sharing in the legal sense, or any other interest in the Company.

6.1 Calculation & Payment

  1. The Partnership Share of 25% shall be calculated on Net Revenue of the Platform collected during each calendar month.
  2. Payment shall be made within fifteen (15) days of the close of each calendar month, accompanied by a detailed revenue statement showing gross revenue, permissible deductions, Net Revenue, and the 25% amount payable.

  1. Payment shall be made by NEFT/RTGS bank transfer to Party B's designated bank account, subject to applicable TDS deductions.
  2. The Company shall maintain accurate and auditable financial records of all Platform revenues. Party B shall have the right, upon seven (7) days' written notice, to inspect or audit such records (or appoint an independent chartered accountant to do so) once per financial year.

6.2 Pre-Revenue / MVP Phase

  1. Prior to the Platform generating commercial Net Revenue, Party B shall not be entitled to any monetary payment under this Agreement unless separately agreed in writing.
  2. The Parties may agree in writing to a nominal stipend or expense reimbursement during the pre-revenue development phase; such agreement shall be documented as an addendum to this Agreement.

6.3 Exclusions from Net Revenue

The following shall be excluded from Net Revenue for purposes of computing the Partnership Share:

  1. GST and all other statutory taxes collected from customers;
  2. Payment gateway and processing charges;
  3. Amounts refunded to customers;
  4. Revenue from non-Platform products or services of the Company.

6.4 Term of Revenue Share

  1. The obligation to pay the 25% Partnership Share shall continue for the full fifteen (15) year Agreement Term, commencing from the date of first commercial revenue from the Platform.
  2. In the event the Agreement is terminated early by the Company without cause, the Company shall pay Party B a Settlement Amount equal to the average monthly Partnership Share paid in the preceding twelve (12) months, multiplied by the number of months remaining in the fifteen (15) year term, discounted at eight percent (8%) per annum (simple interest).
  3. If Party B terminates this Agreement voluntarily or for cause attributable to Party B, the Partnership Share obligation of the Company shall cease as of the termination date, subject to payment of amounts accrued but unpaid.

ARTICLE XI – TERM, RENEWAL & TERMINATION

  1. Term: This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in force for a period of fifteen (15) years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Article.
  2. Renewal: Upon expiry of the fifteen (15) year term, the Parties may mutually agree in writing to extend the Agreement on revised terms. Absent such agreement, the Agreement shall expire automatically.
  3. Termination for Cause: Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party: (a) commits a material breach and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of notice; (b) becomes insolvent or subject to winding-up proceedings; or (c) engages in fraud, willful misconduct, or criminal activity.
  4. Termination for Convenience by the Company: The Company may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon one hundred and eighty (180) days' written notice, subject to payment of the Settlement Amount specified in Clause 6.4(b).
  5. Termination for Convenience by Party B: Party B may terminate this Agreement upon ninety (90) days' written notice. In such event, Party B shall hand over all source code, documentation, and access credentials in a complete and operable condition during the notice period.
  6. Obligations on Termination: Party B shall deliver all Deliverables, revoke all access credentials, and return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Company within fifteen (15) days of the termination date.

ARTICLE XII – DISPUTE RESOLUTION

  1. Amicable Resolution: The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiations within thirty (30) days of written notice of the dispute.
  2. Arbitration: Disputes not resolved amicably shall be referred to binding arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. A sole arbitrator shall be mutually appointed. The seat and venue shall be Bhiwadi (city), India. The language of arbitration shall be English.
  3. Interim Relief: Nothing herein prevents either Party from seeking interim or injunctive relief from a competent court to prevent irreparable harm pending arbitration.
